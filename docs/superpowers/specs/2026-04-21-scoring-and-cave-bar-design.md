# Scoring Display & Cave Bar Design
**Design Date:** 2026-04-21

---

## Scoring Display

### In-Arena
- Floating +X numbers appear above the player when points are earned (e.g. +3 for weak point hit, +5 for perfect dodge)
- Numbers appear immediately at the moment of the event

### End-of-Hunt Summary Screen
- Shown after each hunt before entering the cave bar
- Detailed breakdown per player: damage dealt, weak point hits, perfect dodges, staggers triggered, revives, bonuses earned
- Shows MVP and individual stat highlights
- Any one player can dismiss it — all players advance to cave bar together

### Cave Bar
- Running point total visible contextually when opening upgrade or shop windows
- No dedicated scoreboard screen — totals surface where they're relevant (upgrade wall, weapon rack)
- Scoreboard station shows cumulative scores across all hunts so far

---

## Cave Bar Scene

### Layout (left to right)
| Station | Purpose |
|-|-|
| Trophy Wall | Defeated dinosaurs shown with full art; upcoming boss as glowing silhouette |
| Scoreboard | Cumulative hunt scores, MVP crown, stat highlights |
| Bartender | Purchase cocktail buffs (2 per player per hunt); central social anchor |
| Upgrade Wall | Spend upgrade points on specialization tree (cave paintings) |
| Weapon Rack | Swap or upgrade primary weapon |
| Exit | Ready-up point |

### Navigation
- Gamepad controlled — players move their character freely left to right in the cave bar space
- No time limit — players stay as long as they like
- No menu system — all interactions happen by walking up to a station

### Drop-In / Drop-Out
- New players enter from the far left (cave entrance) and walk in
- Leaving players walk out the exit
- Drop-in/drop-out only possible during cave bar phase — not mid-hunt

### Ready-Up
- Each player signals ready by interacting with the exit
- Exit displays a live "X/Y Ready" indicator visible to all players
- Hunt starts when all active players have confirmed ready
